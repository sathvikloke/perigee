# Perigee

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Motivated learners exploring real astronomical observations, with teachers and astronomy-club organizers as initial adopters. Desktop and mobile browsers must both support the central investigation.

## Product Purpose

Help a newcomer compare SPHEREx observations and explain what the evidence supports. The concrete result is an evidence card containing a claim, selected observations, provenance and uncertainty.

## Positioning

A guided educational comparison workflow joining time, wavelength, quality and the learner's reasoning. Existing viewers and citizen-science tools overlap; no first-ever claim is established.

## Operating Context

A six-person team is building during a two-day NASA Space Apps sprint. The selected challenge requires public coordinate input and chronological imagery. Two investigations use Epsilon Indi B and Barnard's Star; five named presets and general coordinates remain in scope.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React/TypeScript/Vite frontend, Python/FastAPI processing, Vercel hosting. Bounded metadata searches and individual frame requests. The frontend and backend are being implemented concurrently by Claude workers. UI prototypes must identify themselves as prototypes and must not claim live archive behavior before integration.

Observation date and wavelength are separate. Coverage, flags and unknown data remain visible. A catalog prediction is not a detection. No precision photometry, measured proper motion, new discovery, native app or runtime AI is promised. Only Sathvik and Sagar own full raw-data copies.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Perigee. Voice: curious, direct and understandable. The user asks Codex to handle UI design, requires Bloody Coconut for frontend work, and asks that the Obsidian UI design vault inform the result. The saved owner preference favors rounded forms. Avoid repeated headings made of two short punchy sentences.

## Evidence on Hand

The original local SPHEREx FITS collection, source ledgers and a new independent science/content handoff in PR #8. Audience needs and repeat use remain product hypotheses, not measured user results. No visual identity has yet been selected by the user.
