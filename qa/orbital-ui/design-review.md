Disposition: **resolved for the inspected visual scope**. No remaining material visual or task-navigation finding in the reviewed landing and investigation states. This is a design review, not a claim of universal usability or full accessibility.

## Review method and scope

A separate reviewer first inspected the rendered landing and investigation screenshots without the new direction brief, reference screenshots or implementation rationale. ## Stage 2: reference and contract fidelity

The hero preserves the selected recognition-critical hierarchy: compact navigation, small central wordmark, a large centered two-line heading, short supporting copy, stacked actions and a source/status note. Its bounded text measure and quiet reading column survive the phone layout. Sprint provides the clearest settled reference; the supplied Apollo capture includes a transitional blurred headline, limiting final-state detail comparison.

Intentional adaptations are the user-pinned black canvas, white stars, white main type and citron accent; rounded text-only action buttons; and astronomical imagery in the continuation. Apollo's floating navigation shell, peripheral cards and Sprint's terrain are not copied. The user requested their hero format only. No material unexplained hierarchy or proportion drift remains.

The full page advances from an invitation to two concrete investigations, smaller open targets, an example evidence outcome, coordinate search and browser-local saved records. The working bench retains equal registered plates on desktop, a distinct notebook, adjacent dates/wavelengths, explicit flag warnings and catalog-prediction caveats. Phone uses a full-size selected observation and direct access to writing.

## Keep and limits

Preserve the sparse starfield, deliberate heading wraps, real-image cards, rounded graphite surfaces, citron selection/action hierarchy, readable scientific metadata and explicit uncertainty. Further decorative expansion is not needed for this brief.

Still images establish sampled composition and legibility, not orbital direction/timing, pause behavior, backend correctness, scientific provenance, persistence or export fidelity. Those checks and their boundaries are documented in README. This reviewer did not operate a browser or render a PDF. No user study, learning-outcome claim, full assistive-technology audit or cross-browser certification is implied.
