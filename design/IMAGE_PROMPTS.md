# Composition exploration prompts

Generated compositions are UI exploration only. The prototype uses real FITS-derived images instead.

## Option A

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high-fidelity desktop web app UI design comp for PERIGEE, a NASA SPHEREx educational investigation workspace. It is a classroom experiment bench translated into a contemporary browser interface: compare actual observations, check wavelength and quality, write a supported conclusion. No giant marketing hero. Same visual world across alternatives: cool ivory canvas, deep navy type, soft cornflower blue selection, small pale lavender and muted lime supporting surfaces, cobalt actions, rounded 20–24px panel corners with smaller coherent control radii. Humanist sans typography, clear 16px body equivalent, tabular metadata. Restrained purposeful design, readable and distinctive. Header PERIGEE, Explore, Investigations, My evidence. Main question 'Is this a fair comparison?' Target 'Epsilon Indi B'. Images may be represented by simple grayscale star-field placeholders labelled 'Design preview' so they are never mistaken for scientific evidence. Dates Apr 29, 2026 and Sep 27, 2026, wavelength 2.323 μm / 2.322 μm. Controls: compare, previous/next frame, pause, wavelength, quality details. Evidence editor has 'What do you notice?', 'What remains uncertain?', and 'Save evidence'. Include source and quality labels near the image. Show enough of continuation to convey an actual working flow. No NASA logo, false discoveries, fake graphs, neon space gradients, decorative planets, illegible microtype or rasterized application-code screenshot framing. Landscape image around 1536x1024. OPTION A: Central two-up comparison stage occupies 65% width, compact guided steps above the stage, evidence notebook occupies 30% right column. Wide timeline/contact strip directly under images, clean metadata rows. Tiny target selector at top of stage. This is the most operational composition.
