# Submission demo content requirements

Verified 15 November 2026 against the [official Project Submission Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/).

- The Project Demo can be a video of 30 seconds or less, or a slide deck of no more than seven slides, including title/intro. Exceeding those limits prevents Global Judging eligibility.
- The deck and linked materials must be in English.
- Identify team name, every team member, and the selected challenge.
- Explain the project and demonstrate the finished product with readable visuals; the guide specifically asks for an image of the project, the user experience, and how NASA/partner data or technology enable it.
- Host the demo publicly with no login, password, permission request or registration. The form links documents; it does not upload them.
- There is no stated per-slide word maximum. The guide recommends readable type and limiting words where possible. This edition is authored for independent reading, with essential definitions, examples, results and explanations on the slides.
- The guide says most evaluation is based on the written project submission. The detailed page remains necessary even with a fuller deck.
- Attribute external data/resources and keep applicable SI acknowledgments. Generated images/videos require visible indicators. This deck uses actual SPHEREx images and a screenshot of the app, with no generated spacecraft illustration. Project development credits remain in the repository and linked report.
- Demos/final projects may not include likenesses of people under 18, including audio. This deck includes names and scientific/application images only.

Coverage in this edition: slide 1 problem/product/audience/team/challenge; slide 2 actual-app walkthrough; slide 3 all three cases and conclusions; slide 4 data, processing and delivery; slide 5 scoped validation and error tests; slide 6 worked evidence card and traceability; slide 7 use cases, repeat use, app/code/report links.
