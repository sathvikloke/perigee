# Winning-video references for Perigee

Reviewed 15 November 2026. This is a bounded audit of three actual 2025 global-winner videos, separate from the earlier review of winner websites. Their awards are verified by [NASA's official 2025 announcement](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/stem-engagement-at-nasa/nasa-announces-2025-international-space-apps-challenge-global-winners/). We did not obtain judges' scores or explanations of their choices. The recommendations below are our editorial inferences, not a winning formula.

The PureFlow transcript includes an obvious recognition error in the NASA data name, and Resonant's includes an apparent mission-name error. We paraphrase their pitch structure rather than treating automatic captions as an authoritative technical record.

## What this suggests for our story

Perigee's audience should not have to infer who benefits from a list of features. A student sees an apparent shift, needs a way to check it, and leaves with an explanation connected to observations and uncertainty. A teacher can inspect that reasoning. That is a concrete intended benefit; it does not require an invented classroom trial or adoption figure.

The short video should follow **one Barnard's Star investigation**: apparent shift → immediate question → comparison checks → learner-authored conclusion → source-linked evidence card. Show the science image before the branding sequence. The polished landing entrance can appear in a longer presentation, but it should not consume the short demo's opening.

The [official 2022 judging guide](https://spaceapps-prod.s3.amazonaws.com/media/documents/Space_Apps_2022_Judging_and_Awards_Guide.pdf) assesses impact, creativity, validity, relevance and presentation, and explicitly calls for a clear account of the problem, solution and importance. Potential benefit belongs in the pitch; scientific claims still need appropriate support. This historical source is not a substitute for the current competition's rules. Use the current [submission guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/) and the [recording plan](SUBMISSION_VIDEO.md) for submission requirements.

## Additional evidence and limits

- **Photonics Odyssey — 2025 Most Inspirational:** six timed frames from its [115.94-second supporting dashboard recording](https://drive.google.com/file/d/16iRi2CIML7Yi4pElFtbm0AILrFyeWfBX/view) show a landing, administration, network, globe, tactical and device views. This is a supporting prototype tour, **not its submitted short pitch**; the submitted pitch was a deck. It is not counted among the three short videos above.

Its project-page story is not treated as observed video content. Its winner status is confirmed by [NASA's 2024 announcement](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/stem-engagement-at-nasa/nasa-international-space-apps-challenge-announces-2024-global-winners/).
